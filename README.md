# CampusConnect: A Smart Platform for Student Mentorship, Resource Exchange between Peers, and Accommodation Matching
Every academic year, thousands of students transition into new campuses, cities, or hostels—especially first-year students unfamiliar with their surroundings. At the same time, senior students possess valuable resources (textbooks, notes, furniture, local knowledge) and vacate accommodations that go to waste due to the absence of a structured platform connecting these two groups.

Currently, most of this exchange happens informally—through scattered WhatsApp groups, notice boards, word-of-mouth, or last-minute scrambling—leading to several recurring problems:

Lack of Mentorship Access: Junior students have no reliable way to connect with seniors for academic guidance, campus orientation, or general advice, leaving them to navigate challenges alone.
Wasted Resources: Seniors graduating or moving on often discard, dump, or sell books and other academic materials at throwaway prices to random buyers, instead of passing them on affordably to juniors who need them.
Unstructured Accommodation Search: Incoming students struggle to find trustworthy information about hostels, PGs, and rental flats near their campus. There is no synchronised system that tracks when senior students vacate a property (creating availability) and when new students arrive (creating demand), leading to missed opportunities, delayed searches, and reliance on unverified brokers.

There is a clear need for a centralised digital platform that bridges the gap between junior and senior students—enabling mentorship, facilitating fair peer-to-peer deals, and simplifying the accommodation search process by synchronising vacancy and demand cycles with verified broker support.
